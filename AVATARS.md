# Avatars

Your avatar is your name on the scoreboard. It's one animal in one colour, for example **Blue Fox**.

**15 animals × 12 colours = 180 avatars.** You claim one in session 1, first come, first served.
The list of avatars already taken is on the screen, so two people can't have the same one.

## Keep it anonymous

Only you know which avatar is yours, unless you tell someone. **Don't put it in your repository's README**:
that's how other people would find out.

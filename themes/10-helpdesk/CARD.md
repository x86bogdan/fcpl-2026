# Helpdesk / Ticket Tracker

**A small Jira of your own: tickets, states, and rules about how they move.**

You build a ticket tracker: projects, the tickets inside them, who's working on what, and the rules for how a ticket moves from Open to Done. Then the questions a team lead asks every Monday: *"what's overdue?"*, *"who has too much on their plate?"*, *"how long do bugs stay open?"*

- **Your items:** tickets — title, kind, priority, state, assignee, opened and closed dates
- **Where the data comes from:** your own seed file — a made-up team and a made-up product
- **Something happens when:** a ticket is opened, or moves to a new state
- **Parent and children:** a Project has many tickets
- **What you'll look at:** a ticket list with filters, and one ticket's details and history

**Good for you if** you want your project to look like the software you'll be paid to build.

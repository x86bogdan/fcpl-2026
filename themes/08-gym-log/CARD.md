# Gym & Workout Log

**Log it, track it, watch the numbers go up.**

You build a training log: every workout, strength or cardio, grouped into training blocks, with the app spotting your personal records as they happen. Then the questions: *"what's my best squat, ever?"*, *"did this block actually make me stronger?"*, *"how many weeks in a row have I trained?"*

- **Your items:** workouts — date, kind, duration, the main lift or the distance, how hard it felt
- **Where the data comes from:** your own log, written by hand. Optionally [wger](https://wger.de/en/software/api) for an exercise catalogue (free, no key to read)
- **Something happens when:** a workout is logged, or a personal record is beaten
- **Parent and children:** a Training block has many workouts
- **What you'll look at:** your log, searchable, and your records

**Good for you if** you train, or want a reason to start — a new PR is the most satisfying event in the whole catalogue.

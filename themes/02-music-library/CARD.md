# Music Library

**Your music, organised — and finally explained.**

You build a music library: albums and their tracks, what you've played and how often, and a set of questions your streaming app never answers — *"which artist do I actually listen to most?"*, *"how many hours of jazz do I own?"*, *"what haven't I played in a year?"*

- **Your items:** tracks — title, artist, album, genre, length, year, play count
- **Where the data comes from:** your own seed file, optionally enriched from [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/doc/MusicBrainz_API) (free, no key)
- **Something happens when:** a track is added, played, or crosses a play-count milestone
- **Parent and children:** an Album has many Tracks
- **What you'll look at:** a searchable library with track details and your listening stats

**Good for you if** you listen to a lot of music and like the idea of a personal "Wrapped" you built yourself.

# Bookstore Rental

**Who has which book, when it's due back, and what they owe.**

You build the rental system for *BookSmart*, Emma's neighbourhood bookshop: the books on the shelves, who has borrowed which, due dates, late fees, and special orders for books she doesn't stock. Then the questions: *"which books are overdue right now?"*, *"who always brings them back late?"*, *"what earns more, rentals or fees?"*

- **Your items:** books — title, author, kind, year, shelf price, and who has it until when
- **Where the data comes from:** [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/developers/api) (free, no key) — or your own shelf
- **Something happens when:** a book is added, rented, returned, or becomes overdue
- **Parent and children:** a Customer has the books they're renting
- **What you'll look at:** the shelf, with what's out and what's overdue

**Good for you if** you like business software with real rules — dates, money and people who return things late.

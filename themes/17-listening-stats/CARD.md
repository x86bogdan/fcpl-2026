# Listening Stats ("Wrapped")

**Your own year in review, built from your real listening history.**

You build the year-in-review your streaming app shows you once a year — except yours answers any question, any day. Import your listening history, and ask: *"who did I really listen to most, in minutes?"*, *"what do I play at 2 a.m.?"*, *"which artist did I discover this year and never stop playing?"*

- **Your items:** plays — when, what, by whom, how long, skipped or not
- **Where the data comes from:** your own history — Spotify's "download your data" gives JSON — or a history you generate
- **Something happens when:** a play is recorded, or you reach a milestone (1,000th play of an artist)
- **Parent and children:** an Artist has many plays
- **What you'll look at:** the report — your year on one screen

**Good for you if** you love statistics about yourself. It's the most LINQ-heavy theme in the catalogue; the window *is* the report.

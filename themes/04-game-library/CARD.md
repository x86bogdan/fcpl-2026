# Game Library

**Every game you own, what you actually played, and the backlog you'd rather not count.**

You build a game library: what you own, on which platform, how long you've played, what you finished and what you abandoned. Then it answers the awkward questions: *"how many hours is my backlog?"*, *"what did each hour of fun cost me?"*, *"which genre do I always give up on?"*

- **Your items:** games — title, platform, genre, hours played, status, price paid
- **Where the data comes from:** your own library, written by hand. Optionally [RAWG](https://rawg.io/apidocs) for genres and release dates (free, with a key)
- **Something happens when:** a game is added, finished, or abandoned
- **Parent and children:** a Developer has many games
- **What you'll look at:** your shelf, filterable by status, with details per game

**Good for you if** you play games and suspect your backlog is a bigger problem than you admit.

# Pokédex

**Catch them, store them, compare them.**

You build a Pokédex: a catalogue of Pokémon, loaded live from a public API, that you can search, sort, group under their trainers and ask questions of — *"which of my Pokémon is strongest against Water?"*, *"which types am I missing?"*

- **Your items:** Pokémon — species, types, level, stats, nickname
- **Where the data comes from:** [PokéAPI](https://pokeapi.co) — free, no key, over a thousand species
- **Something happens when:** a Pokémon joins the dex, evolves, or levels up
- **Parent and children:** a Trainer has many Pokémon
- **What you'll look at:** a searchable dex with a details panel

**Good for you if** you like games, rich data, and having an answer ready for every LINQ question.

**Heads up:** the course's worked examples are also a Pokédex. You won't be copying them — your kinds, your questions and your feature have to be your own. [The full spec](SPEC.md) says exactly where the line is.

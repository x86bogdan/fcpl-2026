# Plant Care Tracker

**Every plant in the flat, when it needs water, and which one you're slowly killing.**

You build a plant tracker: your plants, room by room, each with its own watering rhythm, and a history of what you actually did. Then the questions: *"what needs water today?"*, *"which room do I neglect?"*, *"am I overwatering the cactus again?"*

- **Your items:** plants — name, species, kind, room, light, last watered, care history
- **Where the data comes from:** your own plants (or a believable invented flat). Optionally [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) so balcony plants skip a day when it rains (free, no key)
- **Something happens when:** a plant is added, watered, or needs water
- **Parent and children:** a Room has many plants
- **What you'll look at:** today's to-do list, and each plant's history

**Good for you if** you have plants, or had plants — the whole app is a system of events, so S6 feels like the point rather than an extra.

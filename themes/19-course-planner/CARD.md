# University Course Planner

**Your own degree, semester by semester — with the clashes and missing prerequisites found for you.**

You build a planner for a university programme — your own is best: every course, its credits, its timetable slots and what it requires first. Then the questions: *"does my timetable clash?"*, *"can I take this elective yet?"*, *"does every semester add up to 30 credits?"*

- **Your items:** courses — code, name, credits, kind, semester, timetable slots, prerequisites
- **Where the data comes from:** your faculty's curriculum, typed in by you
- **Something happens when:** a course is added, or a plan breaks a rule
- **Parent and children:** a Semester has many courses
- **What you'll look at:** the programme, semester by semester, and a weekly timetable

**Good for you if** you want something useful for yourself next semester. Keep prerequisites simple — the full version is a graph problem, and that's a stretch.

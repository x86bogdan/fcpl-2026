# Film & Series Watchlist

**What to watch, what you watched, and what you dropped in episode three.**

You build a watchlist for films, series and anime: what's planned, what you're halfway through, what you finished and what you gave up on. Then it answers what streaming apps won't: *"how many hours until I clear my list?"*, *"which genre do I always drop?"*, *"how much of this franchise have I actually seen?"*

- **Your items:** titles — name, kind, genres, year, runtime, status, your rating
- **Where the data comes from:** [TMDB](https://developer.themoviedb.org) (free, with a key) or [Jikan](https://jikan.moe) for anime (free, no key) — or your own seed file
- **Something happens when:** a title is added, an episode is watched, or a series is finished
- **Parent and children:** a Franchise has many titles
- **What you'll look at:** your list, filterable by status, with progress bars for series

**Good for you if** your "to watch" list is longer than your free time for the next decade.

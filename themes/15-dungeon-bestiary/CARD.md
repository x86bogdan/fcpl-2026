# Dungeon Bestiary

**Monsters, their stats, what they drop — and encounters built from them.**

You build a bestiary for a fantasy world: creatures with stats and weaknesses, the dungeons they live in, what they drop when defeated, and a generator that builds fair encounters. Then the questions: *"what's the most dangerous thing in the Crypt?"*, *"what should a level-3 party fight?"*, *"which monster is worth farming for gold?"*

- **Your items:** creatures — name, kind, size, challenge rating, hit points, stats, loot table
- **Where the data comes from:** the [D&D 5e SRD API](https://www.dnd5eapi.co) (free, no key) — or a world you invent
- **Something happens when:** a creature is added, or defeated
- **Parent and children:** a Dungeon has many creatures
- **What you'll look at:** the bestiary, searchable, with a creature's stat block

**Good for you if** you play tabletop or RPG games — this is Pokédex's cousin, without the branding.

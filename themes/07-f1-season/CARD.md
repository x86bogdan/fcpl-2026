# Formula 1 Season Tracker

**Every race result, and the championship computed from scratch.**

You build a season tracker: races, the result of every driver in every race, and standings your code computes itself — not copied from anywhere. Then the questions: *"who wins the head-to-head between teammates?"*, *"who gained the most places from the grid?"*, *"can anyone still catch the leader?"*

- **Your items:** race results — driver, team, race, grid position, finish, points, or why they retired
- **Where the data comes from:** [Jolpica-F1](https://github.com/jolpica/jolpica-f1) (free, no key — the successor to Ergast)
- **Something happens when:** a result is posted, or the championship leader changes
- **Parent and children:** a Race has many results
- **What you'll look at:** a results table per race, and the standings

**Good for you if** you watch F1, or any sport where the table at the end is the whole point.

# TCG Deck Builder

**Your card collection, and decks that are actually legal.**

You build a deck builder for a trading card game: a collection of cards, decks built from it, and rules that say whether a deck is allowed. Then the questions: *"what does my mana curve look like?"*, *"which cards do I own but never play?"*, *"is this deck legal — and if not, why?"*

- **Your items:** cards — name, type, cost, colour, rarity, power and toughness
- **Where the data comes from:** [Scryfall](https://scryfall.com/docs/api) for Magic: The Gathering (free, no key) — or your own seed file for any card game
- **Something happens when:** a card joins the collection, or a deck stops being legal
- **Parent and children:** a Set has many cards
- **What you'll look at:** your collection, searchable, and a deck with its curve

**Good for you if** you play Magic, Pokémon TCG, Hearthstone or similar, and like rules that are precise.

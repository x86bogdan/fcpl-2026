# Build a Game

**A game of your choice — as long as it runs on data.**

You build a game: a roguelike, a deck-builder, a tower defence, an RPG with an inventory, a management sim. It has to meet the same eleven requirements as every other theme, which means the game needs *content* — enemies, items, cards, towers — with enough variety to query, save, list and serve.

- **Your items:** your game's content — at least 20 things with at least 4 attributes each
- **Where the data comes from:** you design it, in a JSON file
- **Something happens when:** an item is acquired, a level goes up, a wave is cleared
- **Parent and children:** a Level has enemies, a Player has an inventory, a Deck has cards
- **What you'll look at:** an inventory or roster screen — not the game's viewport

**Good for you if** you'd build a game anyway. **Heads up:** Pong, Snake, Flappy Bird and Breakout don't qualify — no content to work with. Unity and Godot don't either. Following a tutorial is fine; you'll be asked to change what it produced. Read [the full spec](SPEC.md) before you choose.
